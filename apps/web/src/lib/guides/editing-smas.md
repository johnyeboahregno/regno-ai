# Editing Subject Matter Agents

Subject Matter Agents are editable from `/app/agents`.

## Activate a built-in SMA

1. Open `/app/agents`.
2. In the **Built-in SMAs** section, click **Activate** on the one you want.
3. It is added to your available SMAs instantly (and can then be edited or deleted like any other).

## Update an SMA

1. Open `/app/agents`.
2. Find the SMA in the **Subject Matter Agents** table.
3. Click **Edit**.
4. Change the name, description, focus tags, disciplines, or languages.
5. Save changes.

The built-in **Base Regno Architect** profile cannot be edited or deleted. Existing jobs keep their
recorded execution history, while new Architect jobs use the latest SMA configuration.
