This case study examines the deployment of a utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) at a precision manufacturing facility located at ITX Z&T Ranch in Texas, USA. The facility produces semiconductor components and advanced electronics, requiring exceptionally stable power quality and zero unplanned downtime. The system utilizes 40 Sungrow ST2236UX battery units paired with 5 Sungrow SC4000UD-MV inverters in a specialized dual-inverter topology designed for industrial ride-through, motor starting support, power quality conditioning, and significant demand charge reduction.

Critical Manufacturing Application

Precision manufacturing demands exceptional power quality. A single voltage sag can destroy in-process semiconductor wafers worth $500,000+. This BESS provides instantaneous voltage support, harmonic filtering, and extended backup power while generating $2-4M annually through demand management and grid services in the ERCOT market.

Dynamic Voltage Restorer (DVR) Operation

The three discharging inverters provide DVR functionality for the entire manufacturing facility:

  • Detection Time: < 0.25ms voltage sag detection
  • Response Time: < 1ms full voltage compensation
  • Compensation Range: 0-50% voltage sag correction
  • Duration: Up to 4.5 hours at full compensation power
  • Harmonic Injection: Active cancellation of grid harmonics
