5. Power Flow Analysis

5.1 Operating Modes

Mode 1: Grid Charging Only

When: Overnight (10pm-6am), ERCOT prices low

  • ERCOT Grid → 8 MW → Battery
  • Plant not operating
  • Charging time: 10-11 hours
  • Cost: ~$0.02-0.04/kWh

Mode 2: Battery Discharge Only

When: Daytime operations, grid outage, or high ERCOT prices

  • Battery → 12 MW → Plant
  • Grid disconnected
  • Runtime: 7+ hours at full power
  • Complete ERCOT independence

Mode 3: Simultaneous Operation

When: Grid available, plant operating

  • ERCOT → 8 MW → Battery
  • Battery → 12 MW → Plant
  • Net battery: -4 MW (discharging)
  • Extends runtime during price spikes

Mode 4: Emergency Island

When: ERCOT grid failure (winter storm, heat wave)

  • Full grid isolation
  • Battery → 12 MW → Plant
  • Critical operations continue
  • 8+ days at sand mine load

5.3 4CP Demand Charge Avoidance (Texas Specific)

4CP Transmission Cost Savings

In ERCOT, transmission charges are based on your demand during the 4 Coincident Peaks (4CP) - the highest 15-minute demand periods in June-September. By discharging from battery during these peaks:

  • Average 4CP charge: $4-8/kW/month (varies by utility)
  • Your peak reduction potential: 420 kW
  • Annual 4CP savings: 420 kW x $6/kW x 12 months = $30,240/year
