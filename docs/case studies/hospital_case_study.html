This case study examines the deployment of a utility-scale Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) at a 500-bed regional medical center located at ITX Z&T Ranch in Texas, USA. The system utilizes 40 Sungrow ST2236UX battery units paired with 5 Sungrow SC4000UD-MV inverters in a dual-inverter topology designed to provide life safety power, extended outage protection, and grid services revenue during normal operations.

Life Safety Critical Application

Hospitals require uninterrupted power for life support systems, operating rooms, and critical care units. This BESS system exceeds NFPA 110 and Joint Commission requirements, providing 24+ hours of critical load backup - far beyond the 96-hour fuel supply requirement for traditional generators. The system provides seamless transfer (<10ms) to protect sensitive medical equipment.

Integration with Existing Generators

BESS Role (Primary)
  • Instantaneous transfer (<10ms) - no blink
  • Covers generator start time (10-15 seconds)
  • Provides extended runtime (11-25+ hours)
  • Clean power for sensitive equipment
  • Grid services revenue during normal ops
Generator Role (Secondary)
  • Long-duration backup (96+ hours with fuel)
  • Recharges BESS during extended outage
  • N+1 redundancy for critical loads
  • Required for code compliance
  • Reduced runtime = reduced maintenance
Hybrid Operation During Extended Outage

During multi-day outages (e.g., hurricane, winter storm), the system operates in hybrid mode:

  1. BESS provides immediate, seamless transfer
  2. Generators start and synchronize within 15 seconds
  3. Generators power loads while BESS recharges (8 MW available)
  4. BESS provides power quality conditioning
  5. Generators cycle on/off to conserve fuel; BESS bridges transitions
  6. Runtime effectively unlimited with fuel resupply

Operating Modes

Mode 1: Normal Grid Operation
  • Grid powers all hospital loads
  • BESS maintains 80%+ SOC (UPS reserve)
  • Participates in ERCOT ancillary services
  • Peak shaving during 4CP periods
  • Discharge inverters synchronized (hot standby)
Mode 2: Grid Disturbance
  • Voltage/frequency excursion detected
  • BESS transfers in <10ms (no blink)
  • Grid-forming inverters supply loads
  • Generator start signal issued
  • Seamless operation - no patient impact
Mode 3: Island Operation (Short)
  • Grid offline, BESS powering loads
  • Generators online within 15 seconds
  • BESS bridges until generators stable
  • Transfer to generator without blink
  • BESS enters recharge mode
Mode 4: Extended Outage (Hybrid)
  • Generators primary power source
  • BESS recharges from generators
  • BESS bridges generator maintenance
  • Load shedding to critical if fuel low
  • 25+ hours critical-only if needed
